## Core Landlord Services Offered
1. **$0 Landlord Commission Rental Placement**: NYC HRA pays 100% of licensed broker fees (up to 15% of annual rent) directly under CityFHEPS rules. Landlords pay $0 in real estate commissions.
2. **HRA Cash Incentives & Bonuses**:
   - **15% Signing Bonus**: Lump-sum payment equal to 15% of annual rent for signing multi-year leases with CityFHEPS voucher holders.
   - **Up to $1,000 Unit Repair Funds**: Reimbursable funds to fix cosmetic or minor HQS/HRA inspection defects prior to lease signing.
   - **Guaranteed Security Deposit Voucher**: Issued directly by HRA upon lease execution.
3. **End-to-End Voucher Paperwork Management**: Joy Chowdhury handles packet submission, income verification, background checks, agency liaison, and direct deposit setup with HRA and NYCHA.
4. **Pre-Inspection Audits**: In-person unit walk-throughs to verify CO detectors, window guards, lead paint disclosures, self-closing doors, and radiator covers prior to city housing inspectors visiting.
